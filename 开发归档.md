# 开发归档（DEV NOTES）

本文件用于归档本模组开发过程中的重要信息：模组定位、创意工坊文案、依赖关系、游戏底层接口、Lua 接口，以及各依赖模组提供的方法与函数，避免开发时频繁查阅官方文档。

## 模组定位

- 本模组是**基于精神创伤（Neurotrauma）及其各类附属的天赋增强模组**，目前适配了 NT 外科扩展（NT Surgery Plus）的外科医生职业。
- 规划是修改**所有职业**的天赋，使其能适应高难环境下的精神创伤内容；当前仅完成外科医生部分。
- 灵感源于「天赋增强[SP]」（非移植），原模组已长期未维护。
- 需要 Lua CS for Barotrauma 支持。
- 为单人模式 / 少人服务器添加了部分较为超模的机制。

### English

This is a talent enhancement mod based on Neurotrauma and its various add-ons, currently adapted for the Surgeon class of NT Surgery Plus. Requires Lua CS for Barotrauma.

The plan is to modify the talents of all classes so they can survive the mental trauma content in high-difficulty environments. Project page: https://github.com/tednved/NT-TalentEnhancement

This mod is inspired by Talent Enhancement [SP]. Thanks to the original author:
Original mod: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2958569302

The talents remain largely identical to those in Surgery Plus and the original mod, with only some talents added, removed, or reordered. To make the Surgeon stand apart from the vanilla Medic, part of the Medic's talent content was removed, keeping only the surgery-related parts.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Extra (somewhat overpowered) mechanics for single-player and small servers:
- When a Surgeon is on the verge of death, they trigger an adrenaline rush, gaining about a minute of "second wind" time that can be used to heal themselves.
- The Surgeon's psychosis resistance is increased to 100%.
- Since the surgery skill from Surgery Plus cannot be boosted beyond 100%, the talent tree instead offers bonus healing effectiveness and other skill bonuses to compensate.
